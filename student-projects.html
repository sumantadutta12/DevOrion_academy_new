Beyond the Tutorial

The thinking behind a great project.

A meaningful build tells the story of how you solve a problem, not just the tools you use.

  1. 01

    Start with a problem.

    Understand who you are building for. Define a clear goal and a useful first version.

  2. 02

    Build. Test. Improve.

    Bring your ideas to life, look for useful feedback and refine the details.

  3. 03

    Tell your story.

    Explain your choices, show the experience and share what you learned along the way.
