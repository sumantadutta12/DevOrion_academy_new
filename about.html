Who We Are

We Are a Career-Focused Technology Academy

DevOrion Academy is an online and offline learning platform designed for the next generation of technology professionals. We focus on practical, industry-relevant training in UI/UX Design, Web Development, Software Development and emerging technologies like AI.

Our goal is simple — to make quality technology education accessible, practical and career-oriented for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Good questions deserve clear answers. Here’s what you need to know before your next step.

01What is DevOrion Academy?

DevOrion Academy is a career-focused technology learning platform. Our programs combine practical training, projects and career guidance in web development, software development, design and emerging technologies.

02Do you provide placement support?

Our career support includes resume preparation, mock interviews, project guidance and placement assistance. Contact the academy to discuss the support available for your chosen program.

03Are the courses suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our learning paths include beginner-friendly foundations as well as advanced topics. Explore the course details to find a program that matches your current experience and goals.

04Are the courses online or offline?

Learning formats depend on the program and batch. Contact our team to confirm the available online or classroom learning options, schedules and location details.
